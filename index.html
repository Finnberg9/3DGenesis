GENESIS 3D

You are not a camera. You are a creature in this ecosystem, with a real genome, real metabolism, and real predators. You will get hungry. You can be eaten.

You start as a newborn and grow up on the same cell economy as everything else. Kill something and you absorb one part of its genome into your own body plan. When you die you are reborn as a newborn of your nearest surviving kin.
